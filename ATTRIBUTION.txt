SEPTUAGINT (LXX) READER: SOURCES AND LICENSES

GREEK TEXT
Henry Barclay Swete, The Old Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint (Cambridge University Press, 1909-1930). Public domain.
Digital transcription:
  - LXX-Swete-1930 by Pasquale Amicarelli, published by Eliran Wong. GPL-3.0.
    https://github.com/eliranwong/LXX-Swete-1930
  - Corrected and merged text from "septuagint-swete" by See The Bible (chadmonahan). GPL-3.0.
    https://github.com/chadmonahan/septuagint-swete
Because the Greek data derives from GPL-3.0 sources, the data files in /data are distributed under GPL-3.0 (LICENSE-GPL3.txt).

ENGLISH TRANSLATION
Sir Lancelot C. L. Brenton, The Septuagint Version of the Old Testament (1851). Public domain.
Digital text via eBible.org, as packaged in https://github.com/ctatum20/brenton-septuagint-data. Page headers and footnotes were removed.

VERIFICATION AND CORRECTIONS
The Greek text was cross-checked against the Open Greek and Latin Project's First1KGreek edition of Swete (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://github.com/OpenGreekAndLatin/First1KGreek . Where it showed a clear typo, the corrected spelling was taken from it or fixed by hand. All 115 changes are listed in corrections-log.csv.

WORD DATA (tap a word)
  - Lemmas: Open Scriptures Septuagint Project, LxxLemmas and GreekWordList (CC BY 4.0). Credit: the Open Scriptures Septuagint Project.
    https://github.com/openscriptures/GreekResources
  - Meanings: STEPBible lexicons by Tyndale House Cambridge (CC BY 4.0), https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data
      TBESG: brief lexicon of extended Strong's numbers for Greek, built on G. Abbott-Smith's Manual Greek Lexicon of the New Testament (public domain).
      TFLSJ: Bible-focused edition of Liddell-Scott-Jones, from the Perseus Digital Library (CC BY-SA 4.0).
    Definitions were shortened and cleaned for display. Material derived from LSJ remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.
How the matching was made: the Open Scriptures lemmas follow Rahlfs' edition, not Swete's. Each Swete word was matched to a lemma by aligning the two texts verse by verse, with a correction pass using the most common lemma for each spelling. No openly licensed per-word tagging of Swete's own text exists. The result is automatic, so an occasional word may be matched to the wrong dictionary entry.

FONT
Cardo by David J. Perry, SIL Open Font License 1.1. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cardo

VERSE NUMBERING
Verses follow Swete's numbering, which is the Greek numbering (for example, the Psalms are numbered as in the Greek Psalter). Brenton's English is matched to the same references.
